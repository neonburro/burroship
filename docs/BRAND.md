# Brand
 
What The Burroship is. The product, the conceit, the structure that
holds everything together. Read this first.
 
---
 
## The one-line
 
The Burroship is a working compound in the Cimarron Range and the
small agency that runs it. We build, deploy, and automate small
operational systems for businesses across the Western Slope.
 
That's the practical description. The longer answer is more
interesting.
 
## What it actually is
 
Two things layered into one.
 
**The agency.** We build sites, custom dashboards, internal tools, and
AI automation. Real client work, mostly across the Western Slope of
Colorado. Sometimes farther.
 
**The compound.** A physical place outside Ridgway in the Cimarron
Range. Hot springs nearby. The structures are still being built.
Eventually a hosted experience.
 
**The vessel.** On top of all that, we are building a public-facing
thing that lives at the website: a live photoreal 3D map of the San
Juan Mountains and an airship that cruises the range continuously.
 
The vessel is the part that makes the work feel like a place rather
than a Slack channel. It's also genuinely useful: each partner town
(Ridgway, Ouray, Telluride, Mountain Village) eventually gets its own
mapped page surfacing real places, real businesses, real people.
 
## The conceit
 
There is a real airship cruising over the San Juans at 18,000 feet,
called The Burroship.
 
The website is its lobby. The Cesium map at `/world/` is the live
cruise. The agency is the crew. The agents are the onboard systems.
The towns are the ports.
 
This is not a metaphor we are trying on. It is the operational reality
of the brand. Talk about the airship as a thing that exists, because
it does.
 
## The three rooms
 
The home page is structured around three operational rooms of the
vessel. Each is one of our three core verbs.
 
### Build · The navigation table
 
Where we read the terrain before building inside it. Strategy,
worldbuilding, planning, design, architecture, systems thinking, the
refinement loop. This is the room where we understand a client's
operation before touching a line of code.
 
Six procedural steps: Tree, Design, Plan, Review, Discuss, Finalize.
 
### Deploy · The engine room
 
Where the systems actually run. Continuous deploys, three
environments (DEV / STG / PRD), real infrastructure made visible. This
room grounds the mythology in reality. It is where "we build software"
becomes "your software is at this URL and it is running right now."
 
### Automate · The bridge
 
Where coordination happens. Six onboard intelligences (the council)
manage signals, hold context, route work, answer when called. Not
assistants. Operational systems. The room where the vessel is steered.
 
The agents: Warbleur (Voice), Cypher (Data), Lyra (Tone), Volt (Flow),
Ion (Memory), Canyon (Terrain).
 
## What we sell
 
In rough order of frequency:
 
- Marketing sites for small businesses in our region
- Custom dashboards and admin systems (replacing spreadsheets and
  legacy tools)
- Internal portals, scheduling systems, CRM-like tools
- AI automation work: voice agents, document processing, workflow
  routing
- The occasional weird thing nobody else builds
 
We do not chase volume. We do not write proposals to people we have
never met. The work tends to come from this valley, from referrals,
and from people who have already seen the airship.
 
## Who we are not
 
- An agency portfolio
- A SaaS company
- An AI consulting firm
- A web3 thing
- A startup raising money
 
We are a small operation, locally rooted, technically capable, and
slightly strange.

## The values
 
These are not posted on a wall. They are how we work.
 
- **Restraint over noise.** One bright thing is worth more than ten
  loud ones.
- **Precision over completeness.** Real measurements, real names, real
  places. Specificity is the brand.
- **Operational reality over marketing language.** Show systems
  running. Do not describe them as "innovative."
- **The mountain is the moat.** We build from here on purpose. Slow
  internet keeps you honest. The view does the rest.
- **Mystery accepted internally, questioned externally.** The airship
  is real to us. If a visitor questions that, we let them. The
  confidence is not in proving it. The confidence is in the work.
