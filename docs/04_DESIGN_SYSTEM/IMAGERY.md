## The principle
 
Imagery anchors the world in real place, real materials, real
thresholds. The strongest image is not generic beauty. It is a calm
but loaded moment: docking, approach, a door opening, a ridgeline,
domes in cold air, warm interior glow.
 
## The organism and the vessel
 
Two distinct canonical entities, per canon Section 2.5, founder
ruling:
 
- **The burro organism** is the being and the soul. Realistic, warm,
  slightly uncanny. Never a mascot. No literal human hands.
- **The Burroship vessel** is a real craft the burros crew.
  Weathered, industrial, heavy. Not a burro in a costume. Its own
  object.
 
The burro is not the ship. The ship is not the burro. The burros crew
the ship. Collapsing either into the other is off-canon.
 
## The accent fingerprint
 
The 90/10 rule: 90% neutral and natural, 10% accent as signal
leakage. The accent is the ownership fingerprint. A Burroship hero or
marketing render with zero accent leakage is off-canon by founder
ruling. The exact accent hue is the one open value, finalized by Volt
in the token layer; imagery uses it as signal leakage regardless of
which fruit hue it resolves to.
 
## Hero doctrine
 
- **Desktop:** wide cinematic landscape, real San Juans terrain,
  negative space for copy, the vessel integrated into the land.
- **Mobile:** a separate portrait composition, not a center-crop.
  One clear focal point. Inherits the canonical vessel silhouette,
  does not reinvent it.
 
## Mood words
 
Alpine observatory. Docked airship. Warm threshold. Architectural
silence. Cold air and warm interior. Restrained signal glow. Luxury
field station. Real landscape, mythic purpose.
 
## The leash
 
Imagery is where atmosphere can run away from operation. The test:
does the image feel like a being inside a real world, or a mascot
advertising a brand? The former is the moat. The latter is the
failure. Protect the former at all costs.
