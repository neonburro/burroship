---
 
This is the section-by-section copy scaffold for the homepage hub.
Section 1 (Hero) is intentionally absent here — it is governed by
`HERO_RENDER_BRIEF.md` and gated on the asset. Sections 2–6 are
buildable now. This is a framework, not final copy: it fixes the
job, the structure, the length, and the tone of each section so
final wording is a small step, not an open question.
 
Each section: one short heading (strong noun, low adjective), one or
two sentences of body, one quiet link. No loud CTA blocks. The
preview earns the click by being interesting.
 
---
 
## Section 2 — The World → `/compound/`
 
- **Job:** establish that there is a real place behind all of this.
- **Heading direction:** a plain noun phrase about place. Not a
  slogan. Something like a stated fact about a field station in the
  San Juans.
- **Body:** one or two sentences. The Compound is a real mountain
  field station, not a metaphor. Capable people gather to build,
  automate, deploy. Warm tone, still restrained.
- **now/next/later:** Now — the place and the concept are real.
  Avoid stating physical amenities that are Later.
- **Link:** quiet, into `/compound/`. Phrased as going deeper, not
  "learn more."
 
## Section 3 — The Intelligence Layer → `/neon-burro/`
 
- **Job:** introduce Neon Burro as the intelligence that does the
  work. Distinct from Burroship. Do not slash-pair them.
- **Heading direction:** a noun about the working intelligence — the
  council, the agents, the systems. Sharp, exact.
- **Body:** one or two sentences. A small disciplined council of
  agents with distinct roles. Operational before poetic. No AI hype
  vocabulary.
- **now/next/later:** Now — the council model and ratified agents
  exist. Next — memory, the Owner/Operator seat. Do not state
  autonomous orchestration as Now.
- **Link:** into `/neon-burro/`.
 
## Section 4 — Operational Surfaces → `/pulse/`
 
- **Job:** show there is a real client-operations layer.
- **Heading direction:** plain and operational. The least mythic
  heading on the page.
- **Body:** one or two sentences. Pulse is where client work is run
  — admin, automation, the operational surface. Legible to humans,
  not internal jargon.
- **now/next/later:** Now — described as the operational layer.
  Next — automation surface. Keep Later out of the Now voice.
- **Link:** into `/pulse/`.
 
## Section 5 — What's Coming Online
 
- **Job:** talk honestly about direction without overpromising.
- **Heading direction:** future-facing but grounded. Not "the
  future of" anything.
- **Body:** two or three short lines, explicitly Later-tagged in
  voice. Deployments, memory, mapping, world-state, automation —
  named as direction the system is evolving toward, never as dated
  promises or "coming soon."
- **now/next/later:** this entire section is Later, and the voice
  must make that unmistakable. No destination link — and that is
  honest, not a missing piece.
 
## Section 6 — Closing
 
- **Job:** invite, do not sell.
- **Heading direction:** an invitation to explore the world. Calm.
- **Body:** one sentence. Not a value proposition, not a signup
  push. The door is open; the capable visitor is welcome to go
  through it.
- **Link:** back into the world (`/world/`) or the strongest single
  destination. One link, not a footer of them.
 
---
 
## The discipline this framework enforces
 
- One voice across all five, tone flexing only within the narrow
  band in `TONE_OF_VOICE.md`.
- now/next/later honored per section — nothing Later spoken as Now.
- The ratified four-distinct naming — no slash-pairing, ever.
- Restraint over reach: short headings, strong nouns, one quiet
  link per section, no CTA walls.
 
Final copy is written against this frame and ratified by the
Owner/Operator. The frame exists so that writing is a small bounded
step, not a blank page — and so the hero, when its asset lands, drops
into a page whose voice is already set.
