This is the applied layer. `VOICE.md` owns the voice itself; this
file governs how it flexes across the four pages without breaking.
 
## The constant: voice does not change
 
Across every page and section the voice is the same: intelligent,
restrained, place-based, calm, exact. Slightly mythic but never
vague. Operational before poetic. Confident without startup hype.
Short headings, strong nouns, low-adjective copy, clear verbs.
 
The voice is the moat. It does not get louder for marketing moments
or softer for technical ones. It holds.
 
## The variable: tone shifts by section
 
Tone is the temperature of the voice, not a different voice. It moves
within a narrow band:
 
| Context | Tone | Stays |
|---|---|---|
| World-entry / hero | most cinematic | still exact, never purple |
| The Compound | warmest — lodge light, hospitality | still restrained |
| Neon Burro | sharpest — systems, doctrine | still place-based |
| Burroship | cinematic at entry, exact in detail | still calm |
| Pulse | plainest — operational, legible | still intelligent |
| Closing | invitational | still not a sales pitch |
 
If a tone shift requires abandoning short declarative sentences,
strong nouns, or the no-hype rule, it has gone too far. The band is
narrow on purpose.
 
## The test, unchanged
 
Would the sentence survive being read aloud in a quiet room by
someone competent and unimpressed? Cinematic does not exempt a line
from this. Warm does not exempt it. The Compound page is warmer, not
softer in the head.
 
## The 80/20 holds per page
 
Every page is eighty percent operational substance, twenty percent
atmosphere at most — including the Compound page, where the
temptation to invert it is highest because the subject is a place.
Warm is a tone applied to substance, never a substitute for it.
 
## now / next / later in the voice
 
- **Now** is stated flatly and confidently. It is true, so it does
  not hedge.
- **Next** is stated as motion: being built, in progress, staged.
  Honest, not coy.
- **Later** is stated as direction, never as a dated promise.
  "The system is evolving toward" — not "coming soon."
 
Overpromising is a voice failure, not just a content one. The voice
does not write checks the now/next/later model says are not cashed.
 
## What this prevents
 
The four-page structure creates four chances to drift: the Compound
into resort brochure, Neon Burro into AI hype, Burroship into product
marketing, Pulse into SaaS boilerplate. This file exists so one voice
holds across all four and tone flexes without snapping.
