## The settled structure
 
This was an open founder decision. It is now closed by ruling. The
entities and their relationship:
 
| Entity | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Compound** | The umbrella world. The core made physical. A luxury incubator and mountain field station in the San Juans where capable people gather to build, automate, and deploy real systems. |
| **Neon Burro** | The intelligence and operational layer. The council, the agents, the doctrine. |
| **Burroship** | The primary digital surface of the Compound world. The main interface, for now. |
| **Pulse** | The operational admin layer for client systems. |
| **Geoship partnership** | The architectural and regenerative habitat layer. |
 
## The rule
 
The Compound sits above the others as the brand layer. It does not
replace Neon Burro or Burroship. They are layers and surfaces of one
world, not competing brands.
 
- Neon Burro is *who does the work*
- Burroship is *where you meet the work*
- The Compound is *where the work physically lives*
- Pulse is *how clients see their slice of the work*
- Geoship is *what the place is built from*
 
## Why this matters
 
Before this ruling, four names competed and no document could say
which was primary without contradicting another. That ambiguity would
have propagated into every downstream asset. The ruling makes the
hierarchy explicit so the website, the decks, the recruiting
material, and the agent system all inherit one consistent structure
instead of inventing it per artifact.
 
## What does not change
 
- The burros are agents with purpose, not mascots. Their home is the
  Neon Burro layer regardless of which surface they appear on.
- The map and the world belong to the Burroship surface but represent
  the Compound.
- The Owner/Operator is a role across all layers, not a person tied
  to one.
 
## Open downstream, not open here
 
This document settles the *architecture*. It does not settle the
*visual identity* of each layer — that lives in the design system and
inherits from here. If the design system ever implies a different
hierarchy than this document states, this document wins.
