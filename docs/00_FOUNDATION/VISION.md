## What this is
 
Neon Burro is a small operating system run by a disciplined council of
specialized agents and one Owner/Operator. It builds, automates, and
deploys real systems for real businesses, and it does so from a
specific place: the San Juan Mountains, Ridgway, Colorado.
 
The work is real. The mythology is an interface to the work, never a
replacement for it.
 
## The thesis
 
Most systems are either tools with no identity or identities with no
operational discipline. Neon Burro is neither. It is a set of
specialized operational identities constrained by governance. That
constraint is the product, not the decoration.
 
Two sentences carry the whole vision:
 
> The council is, at all times, smaller than it appears and more
> disciplined than it sounds.
 
> Implementation must inherit from doctrine, not redefine it.
 
Everything else is detail.
 
## The 80/20 rule
 
Eighty percent of everything is real operational truth: shipped code,
working infrastructure, audited changes, honest status. Twenty percent
is the mythological interface that makes the system legible and
memorable: the burros, the world, the Burroship, the map. The instant
that ratio inverts, the project becomes AI cosplay and the moat is
gone. The discipline is the moat. Not the stack, not the story.
 
## What the system is for
 
To turn capable people and real client problems into shipped,
durable, audited systems, inside an atmosphere that feels like a
mountain observatory rather than a startup or a theme park.
 
## What success looks like
 
- Clients get systems that work and keep working
- Every production change leaves an audit trail
- The doctrine shapes behavior and behavior feeds the doctrine back
- The council stays small, the discipline stays high
- The place stays real
 
## What failure looks like
 
Documented honestly so it can be resisted: mythology drift, tool
lock-in, agent duplication, council bloat, context poisoning,
over-automation, human detachment, and implementation quietly
redefining doctrine. These are named in full in the constitution's
failure-modes work when it is written. Naming them is how the system
stays resilient.
 
## Relationship to everything else
 
This vision is the root. The constitution governs how the council
behaves. The anatomy explains how intelligence becomes action. The
design system controls how it all looks and sounds. The product is
what actually ships. This document does not change; the others
inherit from it.
