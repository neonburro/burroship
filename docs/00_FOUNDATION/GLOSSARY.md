# Glossary
 
**Status:** DRAFT · written fresh from ratified material
**Owner:** Volt
**Inherits from:** Council Core v1 (the source of every definition here)
 
---
 
The moment a system invents vocabulary, it can reason about itself
coherently. These are the canonical terms. Where a term is defined in
the constitution, the constitution is the source of truth and this
glossary is the quick reference.
 
**80/20 ratio** — 80% real operational truth, 20% mythological
interface. Constitutional. Inverting it turns the system into cosplay.
 
**Anatomy** — the nine-layer burro-body model for how intelligence
becomes action. Conceptual, never literal implementation.
 
**Brand shell** — the Skin layer. What an agent looks and feels like
from outside. The transfer layer across environments.
 
**Bus pattern** — the communication pattern where a message is
addressed with explicit from/to headers and broadcast to the relevant
council members rather than point-to-point.
 
**Canonical ownership** — exactly one agent owns each domain of
truth. Others may contribute but the owner is the source.
 
**Council** — the full set of agents plus the Owner/Operator. Smaller
than it appears, more disciplined than it sounds.
 
**Council Core** — the constitution. The versioned doctrine document.
 
**Individual cerebral layer** — an agent's own role-specific
cognition. What differentiates one agent from another.
 
**Owner/Operator** — the founder seat. A role, not a person. Holds
absolute override (Sacred Rule 2). In-world skin: the Pilot (pending
Ion's ruling on the name).
 
**Pineal layer** — the identity spark. Tone, instinct, signature
phrasing. The source of an agent's character consistency.
 
**Sacred Rules** — the seven non-negotiable rules in Council Core
Part I. SR1 real outcomes, SR2 Owner/Operator wins, SR3 sourced over
confident, SR4 discipline over speed, SR5 the 80/20 ratio, SR6 no AI
roleplay, SR7 human atmosphere is the moat.
 
**Shared cerebral layer** — the doctrine and truth substrate every
agent inherits. Not a master agent. A substrate, not a superior.
 
**Shared truth** — a fact ratified into doctrine that all agents
reason from identically.
 
**Signal leakage** — the restrained 10% accent presence (the
ownership fingerprint) against a 90% neutral field. Applies to the
burro organism, the vessel, and the interface.
 
**Skin** — see Brand shell. The Part II layer governing outward feel,
environment adaptation, and transfer.
 
**The Pilot** — proposed in-world name for the Owner/Operator role.
Functional term is Owner/Operator; Pilot is the atmospheric skin.
Pending Ion's anti-drift ruling.
 
**Vessel / the Burroship** — a real craft the burros crew. Distinct
from the burro organism. The burro is not the ship; the ship is not
the burro (Burro Design Canon Section 2.5).
