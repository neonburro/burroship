# The Burroship Vision

Status: WORKING DRAFT v1. This document captures the medium-term
vision for The Burroship. It is intended for collaborator handoff and
for keeping the founder's intent visible while focus stays on Neon
Burro execution.

See `docs/00_FOUNDATION/BRAND.md` (shared with `neonburro` repo) for
positioning, the four-layer frame, and the public mission.

## The picture

The Burroship is the dome — the operating layer that runs Neon Burro
behind the scenes. Agents, mapping, infrastructure, the council, the
memory. Today it powers one studio. In time, it carries others.

This document is for thinking about what *in time* means and what work
gets us there.

## What exists today

- A working agent council (Lyra, Volt, Cypher, Ion, Canyon, Warbleur)
  operating across Neon Burro client projects.
- A Pulse-style admin shell, currently per-client (Cimarron Pulse,
  AnswersMD Pulse, Greenville Pulse, NeonBurro Pulse).
- A mapping layer built on Cesium and Mapbox, with the San Juans dome
  concept and the Million Dollar Highway as a recognized signal path.
- A pantheon of 43 burros with profile pages, each one a character.
- 18 Lab projects showing the studio's range.
- The Burroship's own public-facing site at burroship.com, presenting
  Build · Deploy · Automate as the three rooms of the ship.

That's the foundation. The Burroship is real today. It just isn't yet
*portable*.

## What "in time" means

Phase 2 is making The Burroship portable — onboardable by a second
crew that isn't Neon Burro. Not as a SaaS land-grab. As a deliberate,
small, scarce operator's toolkit for a few hundred high-craft studios
worldwide who want to run like fifty-person teams without becoming
fifty-person teams.

The endgame isn't a billion-dollar SaaS. The endgame is the default
operating layer for a few hundred small high-craft studios, the way
Basecamp became the default for a generation of small teams, but
built for the AI-native, agent-augmented, post-tool-sprawl era.

## What we have to build to get there

Concrete engineering work, not strategy:

1. **Pulse generalization.** The admin shell currently has Cimarron
   logic baked in. The portable version separates the *shell* from
   the *client schema*. Same code, different data, different brand.

2. **Council extraction.** The agents currently know things about
   Neon Burro clients that they shouldn't carry into a new crew's
   pineal. Memory scoping per crew. Knowledge graph partitioning.
   The pineal becomes per-crew, not global.

3. **Onboarding playbook.** Documented process for getting a second
   crew live on the ship. Pricing, scope, timeline, deliverables,
   what they get and what they don't.

4. **One real second crew.** Until a second studio is actually
   running on The Burroship, everything above is theory. Phase 2 is
   not done until a second crew is aboard and reports back.

5. **Decide what travels.** Some patterns will travel. Some won't.
   The crew that runs Neon Burro is calibrated to Tyler's voice and
   the council's specific personalities. A second crew will need to
   establish its own calibration. The portable version of The
   Burroship is the parts that are *not* about a specific crew.

## What this isn't

This isn't a venture pitch. The Burroship doesn't need outside money
to reach Phase 2. The studio funds the work. The pace is set by what
the founder can build alongside paying clients.

This isn't a quick play. Realistic timeline from today to first
external crew aboard: 12-24 months of focused work.

This isn't competitive with Neon Burro. Neon Burro is the proof. Every
client win, every Lab release, every late-night ship is evidence that
the operating layer works. The studio is the demo.

## How they reinforce each other

- Neon Burro proves The Burroship is real. The studio is the
  reference implementation. Nobody trusts a studio-tooling product
  built by people who don't run a studio.
- The Burroship makes Neon Burro durable. Agency businesses have a
  ceiling — the founder's time. The Burroship gives Neon Burro a
  second revenue line that scales without burning the founder out,
  and a reason to exist beyond the next client engagement.

The mythology layer (burros, NBR, the council, the airship metaphor)
sits comfortably across both. If anything, the whimsy is the
differentiation in a category that takes itself too seriously.

## My honest take, written for the collaborator

If you're reading this because someone handed you the doc — the bet
here is The Burroship. Neon Burro is already great and will keep being
great. The Burroship is the thing that could be much bigger than it
currently is, and the founder is closer to it than most founders are
when they decide to swing.

The studio gives runway, cred, and a test environment. The ship is
what carries everything out.

What I'd want a collaborator to think about:

1. What does the smallest possible portable version of Pulse look
   like? What can we strip out before we lose the magic?
2. Who is the right first external crew? What relationship does
   that founder have to have with the Neon Burro founder for the
   first onboarding to go well?
3. What's the pricing posture? Premium and high-touch (like Basecamp
   was in its early years) or productized and self-serve (like Linear)?
4. How do we keep the council's personality coherent across two
   crews? Is the pineal shared between crews, or fully isolated?
