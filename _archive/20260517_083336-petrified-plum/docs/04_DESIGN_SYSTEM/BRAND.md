## Governing principle
 
The burros feel like beings inside a world, not mascots advertising a
brand. That distinction is everything. Every rule in this canon exists to
protect that feeling.
 
The atmosphere target is a mountain observatory, not an esports team.
Quiet, calm, observant, slightly uncanny, warm, intelligent. Never loud,
never saturated, never costume-heavy.
 
This canon is the visual expression of Sacred Rule 7 (human atmosphere is
the moat) and the 80/20 ratio applied to character design as a 90/10
ratio: ninety percent real burro, ten percent neon signal.
 
---
 
## 1. The two forms
 
The system has exactly two visual forms. They are not alternatives. They
are a hierarchy.
 
### Form A • The canonical organism
 
The realistic burro. This is the soul, the body, the skin layer, the
emotional anchor. It is what makes Neon Burro feel human, warm, slightly
strange, alive, premium, and grounded.
 
Reference anchor: a realistic burro with tactile fur, grounded posture,
restrained wardrobe, melancholy intelligence, subtle humor, and one
symbolic companion. Editorial illustration, not animation.
 
### Form B • The operational mark
 
The neon line-art burro. This is the badge system, not the organism. It
is the gravatar, favicon, agent badge, deployment mark, app identity, and
UI signature. It works because it compresses the organism into
recognizable signal geometry.
 
Form B never replaces Form A. Form A is the being. Form B is its mark.
 
---
 
## 2. Canonical organism rules
 
These rules govern Form A, the realistic burro body.
 
### Anatomy
 
- Realistic burro anatomy and proportions
- No literal human hands. This is a unanimous council rule. Human hands
  push the character into children's-book anthropomorphic territory and
  weaken the atmosphere immediately
- Grounded posture, quadruped-capable even when seated upright
- The burro remains fundamentally a burro. The realism is the thing that
  makes it emotionally compelling
 
### Fur and material
 
- Tactile, textured fur with visible direction and depth
- Matte, earthy base palette
- Material realism over cuteness. The premium feeling comes from
  restraint, texture, composition, silence, and materiality
 
### Wardrobe
 
- Restrained identity shell only, never costume clutter
- One garment maximum as an identity marker (the tweed-jacket register)
- Wardrobe signals role and environment without forcing human posture
- No costume bloat. No accessory pile-up
 
### Companion
 
- At most one symbolic companion or glyph (the canary register)
- The companion is an accent, not a focal contest with the burro
- Optional per agent, not mandatory
 
### Expression
 
- Subtle. Melancholy intelligence. Quiet humor allowed
- No exaggerated smiles. No mascot energy
- Slightly uncanny is correct. Loud is not
 
### The 10% neon
 
The neon is signal leakage, not the primary material. It appears only as:
 
- Subtle eye glow
- Faint mane edge-light
- Restrained cyan or lime reflection
- Occasional system aura at very low intensity
 
That is the entire neon budget. Nothing more. Restraint creates the
sophistication.
 
### Reference family
 
The defensible lineage, per council research:
 
- Tactile editorial illustration
- Premium natural-history surrealism
- Quiet fashion portraiture
- Restrained Scandinavian character work
- Cinematic storybook realism
 
Explicitly NOT: animated mascot culture, Pixar, esports iconography,
cyberpunk saturation.

## 2.5 The organism and the vessel
 
Founder ruling, 2026-05-17. This section resolves the question the
first Burroship render forced into the open, and closes the
corresponding Part X §38 tension by founder decision.
 
### Both exist, and they are distinct
 
There are two canonical entities, not one:
 
1. **The burro organism (Form A).** The being. The soul. The
   realistic burro defined in Section 2. This is the emotional
   anchor and the identity of every council member.
2. **The Burroship (the vessel).** A real craft. A constructed
   airship the burros crew and inhabit. It is not a burro. It is
   not Form A wearing a costume. It is its own object with its
   own industrial design language.
 
The burro is not the ship. The ship is not the burro. The burros
crew the ship. This is the settled relationship.
 
### Why this matters for the canon
 
This prevents two failure modes:
 
- Collapsing the ship into the organism, which would dilute the
  burro's role as the soul and turn it into a vehicle mascot
- Collapsing the organism into the ship, which would lose the
  warm, slightly uncanny being that is the actual moat
 
The organism carries warmth and identity. The vessel carries
scale, arrival, and operational gravity. They do different
emotional jobs and must remain visually distinct.
 
### Vessel design language
 
The Burroship vessel inherits the same atmosphere discipline as
the organism, even though it is not a burro:
 
- Weathered, industrial, heavy, real. Not glossy concept-car
- Cold mountain-dusk lighting, San Juans terrain truth
- Mandatory lime signal leakage as the ownership fingerprint
  (see Section 2 neon rule, now mandatory not optional)
- "Mountain observatory, not esports team" applies to the ship
  exactly as it applies to the organism
- One vessel silhouette becomes canonical once a hero asset is
  locked. Future renders inherit that silhouette, they do not
  reinvent it per image
 
### The lime fingerprint is now mandatory
 
Section 2's 10% neon ceiling stands. What changes by founder
ruling: a Burroship asset with zero lime signal leakage is now
off-canon. The faint lime is the ownership signature. Without it
the vessel reads as a generic luxury craft. The 10% is a ceiling,
not a target, but it is no longer allowed to be zero on hero or
marketing renders.

## 3. Operational mark rules
 
These rules govern Form B, the neon line-art badge.
 
- Minimal line geometry, single continuous-feeling stroke language
- Must survive favicon size (16px) without losing identity
- Works as avatar, app icon, deployment mark, system token
- Neon palette permitted here at higher intensity than Form A, because
  this is a signal token, not the organism
- Black or near-black field default
- No fill, no gradient mesh, no 3D. Flat, sharp, premium
- The mark is the compressed organism, never a different creature
 
---
 
## 4. Per-agent variation rules
 
Each council member gets a Form B badge. Variation is deliberate and
bounded. The goal is recognizable family with subtle individual
identity, like an app-icon set, never a costume box.
 
### Variation axis (bounded)
 
Permitted per-agent variation, in order of preference:
 
1. **Accent color** • the primary differentiator. Each agent owns one
   signal color
2. **One feature toggle** • glasses or no glasses, mane variation. One
   feature only, not a stack
3. **Nothing else.** Same base structure, same proportions, same stroke
   language
 
Prohibited variation:
 
- Multiple simultaneous feature changes per agent
- Per-agent anatomy changes
- Per-agent style changes
- Loud or saturated treatments that break family coherence
 
### Founding council badges
 
Mapped to Part IX charters. Accent colors proposed, Tyler ratifies:
 
- **Warbleur** • implementation lead. Accent: Topo Lime. Feature:
  baseline mark, no glasses. The custodian reads as the default form
- **Volt** • research and strategy. Accent: electric cyan. Feature:
  glasses (the scrutiny/verification signal)
- **Ion** • synthesis and possibility. Accent: warm amber. Feature:
  mane variation (the possibility/drift-sensing signal)
 
Future agents receive a new accent color and at most one feature toggle
on induction, recorded in their Part IX charter.

## 5. Multi-environment skin behavior
 
The realistic organism is the stable being. Environments modify the
shell around it without changing the organism. This is why the realistic
system supports the multi-environment future better than a fully
stylized system would.
 
Environment-modifiable layers:
 
- Wardrobe
- Palette temperature
- Posture register
- Atmosphere and lighting
- Signal intensity (within the 10% ceiling)
- UI treatment and typography rhythm
- Edge lighting
- Interaction behavior
 
Never modifiable across environments:
 
- Core burro anatomy
- The no-human-hands rule
- The 90/10 neon ceiling
- The being-not-mascot principle
 
The same council member can inhabit Burroship, Neon Burro, Pulse, and
client systems and remain recognizable because the organism is constant
and only the shell adapts.

## 6. The Vitruvian Burro
 
A planned doctrine companion visual, not a mythology artifact.
 
### What it is
 
An operational anatomy visualization that maps the nine organs of Part
II onto a proportioned realistic burro figure. Labeled organs, routing,
layers, ownership, cognition, memory, governance, skin, spine. Clean
technical presentation in the style of an anatomy plate.
 
### What it is not
 
- Not sacred-symbol iconography
- Not worship energy
- Not self-serious mysticism
- Not mythology for its own sake
 
### The line
 
Ion's charter makes Ion the anti-roleplay leash, and Ion drew this line
explicitly: the Vitruvian Burro works only if it remains doctrinal. If it
becomes a sacred symbol it drifts into self-seriousness immediately. If
it stays an anatomy visualization of the doctrine structure, it is
brilliant.
 
### Sequencing
 
The Vitruvian Burro is drawn in the finalized canonical style, after
this canon is ratified. Canon first, Vitruvian second. Same discipline
as doctrine before implementation.

## 7. Do not
 
A consolidated prohibition list. These are the fastest ways to destroy
the atmosphere.
 
- Do not use literal human hands on the canonical organism
- Do not exceed the 10% neon ceiling on Form A
- Do not add costume bloat or accessory pile-up
- Do not use exaggerated smiles or mascot expressions
- Do not make every agent visually loud (this destroys hierarchy
  instantly)
- Do not drift toward Pixar, animated mascot culture, or esports
  iconography
- Do not let Form B (the mark) become a different creature than Form A
- Do not let the Vitruvian Burro become sacred-symbol mythology
- Do not personalize per-agent badges beyond one accent color plus at
  most one feature toggle
- Do not change core anatomy across environments
- Do not collapse the burro organism into the vessel or vice versa
  (Section 2.5, founder ruling)
- Do not ship a Burroship hero or marketing render with zero lime
  signal leakage (Section 2.5, founder ruling)
- Do not reinvent the vessel silhouette per render once a canonical
  hero is locked

## 8. Relationship to the constitution
 
This canon is an implementation artifact. It inherits from Council Core
v1 and never amends it.
 
- Council Core Part II Section 5 defines skin as the brand shell and the
  transfer layer. This canon specifies how that shell looks and behaves
- Council Core Part IX charters define each founding member. This canon
  specifies their visual identity marks
- Council Core Sacred Rule 7 protects human atmosphere. This canon is
  the visual enforcement of that rule
- The 90/10 neon ratio is the character-design expression of the 80/20
  operational ratio from Part I Section 3
 
If this canon and Council Core ever conflict, Council Core wins. The
constitution defines laws. This canon implements one of them.
